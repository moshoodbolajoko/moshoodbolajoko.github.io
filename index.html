User story with Gherkin acceptance criteria

Sample entry from the product backlog — every feature was broken into a user story with Given/When/Then acceptance criteria before build.

US-04.1 — Booking Enquiry Form Must Have

As a visitor, I want to complete and submit a booking enquiry form on a package page without any payment step, so that I can express my interest and the operator can contact me to arrange my booking.

GIVEN I am on a package page and click "Book Now / Enquire"
WHEN the form loads
THEN I see fields for full name, email, phone, preferred dates, party size, and package of interest

GIVEN I complete all required fields and submit
WHEN the form processes
THEN I see an on-screen confirmation and no payment step is presented

04 — Lessons Learnt

Captured as a project team during the reporting period — a working discipline of reflecting on what worked and what didn't.

TypeObservationAction taken
Could do better Technical issues with key project tools delayed completion of otherwise-finished activities and stage closure. Confirm tool access and functionality early; escalate technical issues promptly.
Could do better Delays in resolving dependencies affected planned stage completion dates even when most deliverables were done. Track critical dependencies closely; escalate unresolved blockers early.
Worked well The team completed and gained approval for most Initiate-stage deliverables during the reporting period. Maintained the same approach to deliverable completion, review and approval.
Worked well A project-planning tool issue was identified, escalated to the programme office, and actively followed up. Continued timely escalation and monitoring of technical dependencies.
